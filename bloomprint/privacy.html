Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

Who operates Bloomprint

Bloomprint is operated by Saber Zou. For privacy questions or requests, contact saberzou@gmail.com.

Photos and AI painting

When you request a print, Bloomprint sends a processed version of your selected or captured photo to our server hosted by Cloudflare. Our server sends the photo to OpenAI to check the subject, create an illustration, and generate its title and, for plants, botanical information. It may also verify the illustration against the photo and repaint it. Photos can contain personal information, including people or places. Only send images you have permission to use.

The live viewfinder uses Apple's on-device image analysis. Browsing your existing prints does not itself request a new AI painting. Bloomprint does not send your entire photo library to the painting service.

Where prints are saved

The original photo, illustration and card information are saved in the app. When iCloud is available and configured, these records sync through your private iCloud database to your devices. If you save or share a print, copies are handled by Photos or the destination you choose.

Service identifiers and purchases

Our service processes an app-generated device identifier, daily print counts, timezone offset and request identifiers to apply print limits and recover interrupted jobs. Apple App Attest uses a device-generated key to help verify genuine app requests; the private key remains on your device. If you subscribe, the app sends the subscription's original transaction identifier to our server so we can verify access with Apple. Apple handles payment details; Bloomprint does not receive your card number.

Retention

The current server implementation removes the submitted photo after processing and schedules deletion of job images and results approximately one hour after processing starts. Cleanup depends on successful execution of the hosting service. Print-count records, subscription-status records and registered public attestation keys currently have no automatic deletion schedule. These records remain in our service storage unless removed through service maintenance or a verified deletion request. Contact us to request deletion; we may retain records needed for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations.

OpenAI processes API data under its own service terms and data controls. API data is not used to train OpenAI models by default. Provider-side abuse-monitoring retention can apply, including up to 30 days under standard controls, with exceptions for safety and legal obligations. We do not claim Zero Data Retention. See OpenAI's API data controls. Cloudflare and Apple may also process service and security information under their policies.

Tracking and advertising

The current app includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDK and does not use the advertising identifier. Service identifiers are used for app functionality and abuse prevention, rather than cross-app advertising.

Your choices and deletion

You can decline camera access and choose a photo through Apple's photo picker. Delete an individual print in its detail view, or use Settings → Empty all boxes to remove your app records. With working iCloud sync, deletions propagate to your connected devices. Copies previously saved to Photos or shared elsewhere must be deleted separately. Deleting a print does not automatically erase service usage or subscription records; contact us for those requests. Cancel subscriptions in your Apple account's subscription settings; deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Security, international processing and children

App-server communication uses HTTPS. Cloudflare, OpenAI and Apple may process information outside your country. Bloomprint is not designed specifically for children. Do not upload sensitive information or photos of others without appropriate permission.

Updates and contact

We will update this page when these practices change. Questions, access or deletion requests: saberzou@gmail.com.
