Bloomprint Support

Printing

Printing requires an internet connection. Keep Bloomprint open while the painting develops. If a request fails, check the connection and try again.

Bloomprint Plus

Plus offers a higher daily print allowance. Available prices, billing periods and any eligible introductory offer appear before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled through your Apple account. Use Settings → Restore purchases after reinstalling or changing devices.

iCloud and your prints

Sign in to the same iCloud account on your devices and enable iCloud for Bloomprint. Sync depends on iCloud availability. Prints saved separately to Photos remain there if you delete a print from Bloomprint.

Contact

Send your app version, iOS version and a description of the problem to saberzou@gmail.com. Avoid sending private photos unless necessary and you choose to do so.
