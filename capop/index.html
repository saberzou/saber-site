From line to color

Every page starts as clean lines.

Pick a marker and color on toothy paper. Lock the lines and your stroke stays inside each shape; unlock them and go freehand. Tap a marker again to switch between fine, medium and broad tips.

23 acrylic colorsThree tip sizesStay inside the linesUndo any stroke

Listen

Scritch, scratch.

A marker should sound like a marker. Capop plays a soft, papery scratch that follows the speed of your hand, quiet when you slow down, brighter when you sweep. Turn it off whenever you like.

Put a record on

Six records to color along to.

Tap the record button and the desk turns into a turntable. Pick a sleeve and the record slides out and lands on the platter. Every track is original, made for slow afternoons.
