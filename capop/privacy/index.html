Capop

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

Capop is a coloring studio by Saber Zou. You don't need to create a Capop account. Capop does not include advertising, tracking, or analytics.

Drawings and settings

Capop keeps your drawings, thumbnails, and preferences in the app's storage on your device. They aren't sent to the developer. Coloring pages, reference art, and music are included in the app.

Sharing

When you choose Share, Capop prepares the export on your device and opens the system share sheet. You decide whether to save the file or send it to a destination of your choice.

Purchases

Apple handles payment, purchase verification, and restoration through the App Store. Capop reads the purchase entitlements Apple provides so it can unlock the books or features you own. Capop does not receive your payment card details.

Deleting your data and contacting us

Use Start over to clear the paint from an individual page. Deleting Capop from your device removes the app's local data; copies you exported or shared are separate.

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request. For privacy questions or to request deletion of a support conversation, email saberzou@gmail.com.
