Capop

Support

Need a hand? Email saberzou@gmail.com. If you're reporting a problem, include your device model and the app version shown in Settings.

Restore a purchase

On the shelf, open Settings and choose Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account that you used to buy the book or feature.

Marker sounds and music

Marker sound is in Settings and in the coloring page's More menu. Records are separate: open the Music button and choose a sleeve or tap Play. Your device's media volume controls how loud Capop sounds.

Save a drawing

Open your drawing and tap Share. You can save a copy or send it to another app. Deleting Capop removes drawings that are stored only in the app.
