Make room for yourself.

A small routine, with familiar koi for company.

01

Focus

One thing at a time.

Choose a 1–60 minute timer. Review seven- and thirty-day session totals in Settings.

02

Breathe

Follow a gentle rhythm.

Choose your breathing session in the app, or open one minute from a widget. Breathing pauses when you leave the app.

03

Sleep

Settle down with sound.

Choose a 15–60 minute timer with bundled music or nature sounds. Switch sounds during the session; audio fades in the final minute.

04

Pond

Care for your quiet place.

Completed timers earn food. Feed koi and collect residents and plants. Widgets show saved pond images; Focus/Sleep Live Activities offer session and Sleep music controls.
