Daypond privacy

How the app handles local session data and optional support messages.

Draft for review — not published

These local pages are release-preparation drafts. Public availability and final policy wording have not been approved for publication.

1. Local app data

Daypond stores session timing/history, food and collection progress, pond settings and music preferences locally. It does not send these records to the developer, require an account, or include advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. Widgets and Live Activities

Limited session state and saved pond images are shared with Daypond’s own widget extension on the same device. Session state and music can appear on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island. You can control Live Activities in iOS settings.

3. Permissions and sound

The app plays bundled audio; it does not record your microphone or request camera, Photos, location, contacts, HealthKit or tracking access. iOS controls any Live Activity availability prompts.

4. Retention and deletion

Local history and settings remain until removed with the app or changed through its controls. Three recent cached pond images are retained. Shared-group data and Apple-managed backups/restores follow OS rules; there is no developer-managed cloud copy or remote deletion service.

5. Optional support

Emails to saberzou@gmail.com are received through Gmail and used to answer your request. Send only information you wish to share. Messages may be retained as needed to handle the request and operational or legal obligations; email the same address to ask about access or deletion. This draft retention wording needs final owner review.

6. Website and platform services

These drafts load no scripts, analytics or advertising. When published, hosting services may process technical connection information such as IP address, request time and browser details. Existing hosting can inject analytics scripts; the page policy blocks scripts. Actual deployed behavior still requires verification before publication. Gmail and Apple services operate under their own policies.
