Why does breathing pause?

Breathing needs the app in the foreground so its visual cues and haptics stay with your session.

Why is Sleep paused?

Headphone removal, audio interruption or relaunch can pause Sleep. Open the app and resume explicitly. Your music choice is retained.

Why is the widget image older?

Pond widgets show saved snapshots. Open Daypond for a new capture; iOS controls refresh delivery. Widgets do not continuously animate.

Why is there no Live Activity?

Check Daypond Live Activities in iOS settings. In-app sessions remain available if activities are denied. Widget starts may require unlocking.

Where is my history?

Session history, food, collections and preferences live in local app storage. There is no app-managed cloud account or sync. Apple manages OS backups and restores.

How can I remove local data?

This version has no dedicated erase command. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local app container. Shared widget data and OS backups follow separate Apple retention rules. Save anything you want to keep first.
