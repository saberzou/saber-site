Meet Finwords ↓

Small sessions, familiar company.

From your bowl to the world around you, there’s more than one way to find a word.

01

Bowl

Make words part of your day.

Start with up to five new words, then practise with choice and spelling quizzes. Spaced review brings words back as you learn.

02

Words

Keep the words you find.

Look up words manually, explore bundled definitions and examples, and listen with Apple’s speech synthesis.

03

Things

Name the things around you.

Explore everyday objects or use the camera to recognise an object on your iPhone. Keep a cutout in your collection.

04

Me & Fish

Find your own rhythm.

Choose your learning level, goals and pace. Give yourself and your fish a name, and make the experience your own.

Your next word can wait for you.
