Privacy, in plain words.

How Finwords handles learning records, camera images and optional iCloud sync.

Draft for review · 13 October 2026 · Not yet effective

This draft describes the current pre-release iPhone build. It needs developer approval and release verification before publication. It is not legal certification.

1. Who provides Finwords

Finwords is developed by Saber Zou (邹颂兵), an independent developer. For privacy questions or requests about support correspondence, contact saberzou@gmail.com. This policy covers the Finwords iPhone app and these Finwords web pages.

2. Learning records and settings

The app saves your vocabulary, definitions, examples, learning and review state, and associated dates on your device. Your profile includes the names you choose for yourself and your fish, learning level, goals, pace and helper language. Things progress and device preferences are also stored to provide the app’s features. The current build has no Finwords account, developer backend, advertising, tracking, analytics SDK or third-party crash-reporting SDK.

3. Camera images and recognition

Camera access is requested when you use object recognition. Images are processed on your iPhone using Apple frameworks and the bundled TinyCLIP model. We found no transmission to a developer server or third-party inference service in the reviewed build. When you keep a recognised object, the app saves its name, date, optional catalog identifier and cutout image in app storage. Saved images can also sync through iCloud as described below. Camera permission can be changed in iPhone Settings. The camera feature recognises objects, not text.

4. Optional private iCloud sync

If your device’s iCloud configuration and the app’s entitlements allow it, SwiftData is configured for automatic CloudKit sync of words and saved scans, including cutout images, to your private iCloud database. Profile and Things progress use Apple’s iCloud key-value storage. These services are operated by Apple under your Apple Account and settings; Finwords does not ask you to create a separate account. A local storage fallback exists. Production schema and sync across two devices are not yet verified, so this draft does not guarantee sync or recovery. Apple may process this data to provide its services.

5. Retention, deletion and export

In Words, opening a word card, tapping the trash button and confirming deletes that word record, including its encoded review history. Saved word deletions are intended to sync through automatic SwiftData CloudKit; actual deletion across devices has not been tested. Deleting a word does not delete the separate saved scan record, its cutout image file or cached image copies. Your profile, fish name and Things progress remain in device settings and iCloud key-value storage. The current app has no in-app saved-scan deletion, Things progress deletion or reset, Delete All or complete export control. Replaying onboarding is not a reset. Records otherwise remain available for continued use. Uninstalling does not necessarily remove iCloud records or system backups, and reinstalling may restore synced data. These copies need separate management through Apple’s available controls. We cannot read or delete device-only records through a developer server. Contact us for help understanding the available controls.

6. Support email

If you email us, your email address and the content you choose to send are processed by our email service and the developer to respond and resolve the issue. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. Ask us to access, correct or delete personal information in support correspondence; we will consider the request and applicable retention requirements. App records on your device and Apple’s services are separate from these emails.

7. These web pages

These Finwords pages use local assets and system fonts. They include no analytics script, chat widget, tracking cookies or embedded third-party media. The hosting provider may process connection information such as your IP address and browser details to serve and secure the website. The main Saber Zou portfolio is a separate page that loads Google Fonts and offers an optional chat service; its network behaviour is not represented by these Finwords pages. Email links open your email application.

8. Children and changes

Finwords has no public profile or communication between users. Children should use it with guidance from a parent or guardian and avoid sharing personal information in support requests. Guardians can contact us with privacy questions. If the release build changes how data is handled, this draft and its effective date must be updated before publication.
