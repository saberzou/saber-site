Privacy, in plain words.

How Finwords handles learning records, pictures saved by earlier versions, and optional private iCloud sync.

Policy for Finwords 1.1 · Published 16 November 2026

It applies when you use version 1.1; the archived policy remains available for version 1.0. This policy describes Finwords 1.1 for iPhone, with Bowl, Words, and Me & Fish. The camera/Scan feature and Things tab are no longer offered in this version. If you still use 1.0, refer to the archived 1.0 policy for its camera and Things features.

Archived privacy policy for Finwords 1.0

1. Who provides Finwords

Finwords is provided by Codesigner Tech Co., Ltd. and developed by Saber Zou (邹颂兵). For privacy questions or requests about support correspondence, contact saberzou@gmail.com. This policy covers the Finwords iPhone app and these Finwords web pages.

2. Learning records and settings

Finwords saves your words, definitions, examples, review history and related dates on your device. Your profile includes the names you choose for yourself and your fish, learning level, goals, pace and helper language. Device preferences are also stored. The fish’s diary is assembled on your device from these existing learning records; it does not send them to an AI or developer server. This build has no Finwords account, developer backend, advertising, tracking, analytics SDK or third-party crash-reporting SDK.

3. Pictures saved by earlier versions

This version does not offer camera scanning or object recognition. Pictures and scan records previously saved in 1.0 are not automatically erased by updating. A word may continue to display its earlier cutout picture. The app retains compatible storage, migration and deletion support for these records, including local image files and private iCloud data. It does not upload them to a developer server or third-party inference service. The archived 1.0 policy explains how the earlier camera feature processed images.

4. Optional private iCloud sync

When iCloud is available, words and retained earlier scan records, including their images, can sync through your private iCloud database. Your profile uses Apple’s iCloud key-value storage. Earlier Things progress may remain in storage, but this version no longer reads or updates it; Delete all my data attempts to remove those retired keys. Sync depends on your Apple Account, device settings, connectivity and Apple’s service availability and may be delayed. Core learning works locally without a Finwords account. Sync is not a substitute for a backup. Apple processes data to provide its services.

5. Retention, deletion and export

In Words, open a word card, tap the trash button and confirm to request deletion of the word and review history. If the word uses an earlier scan picture, the app also attempts to delete its linked scan record and that device’s image file and memory cache. Other retained scans are not included in that request.

Me & Fish → Settings → Delete all my data attempts to delete words and retained scan records, save those changes, remove the profile and retired Things keys from private iCloud storage, and clear pictures, settings and pending reminders on the device where you perform it. If the record save fails, later cleanup may not run. Individual-word saves and image-file cleanup can also fail without a visible error. Check the result; contact support if records or pictures remain.

When successful, record deletions can propagate through CloudKit and profile/key removals through iCloud key-value storage, subject to Apple Account, connectivity and sync availability. These are not a remote wipe: deletion received from another device does not automatically purge that device’s cached image files, device-only settings or reminders. For local cleanup, use Delete all my data on each device where you used Finwords, check the result, and contact support if it cannot complete. Synced record copies on offline or delayed devices can remain until synchronization succeeds.

This does not erase Apple system backups or support emails. There is no complete in-app export. Replaying onboarding or uninstalling alone does not necessarily delete private iCloud records. We cannot read your private app records through a developer server.

6. Support email

If you email us, your email address and the content you choose to send are processed by our email service and the developer to respond and resolve the issue. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. Ask us to access, correct or delete personal information in support correspondence; we will consider the request and applicable retention requirements. App records on your device and Apple’s services are separate from these emails.

7. These web pages

These Finwords pages use local assets and system fonts. Their source includes no analytics script, chat widget, tracking cookies or embedded third-party media. The existing website is served through GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. Cloudflare can inject a web-analytics script into the delivered page; these Finwords pages set a Content Security Policy that blocks script execution and outgoing connections. Hosting providers may still process connection information such as your IP address and browser details to serve and secure the website. The main Saber Zou portfolio is a separate page that loads Google Fonts and offers an optional chat service; its network behaviour is not represented by these Finwords pages. Email links open your email application.

8. Children and changes

Finwords has no public profile or communication between users. Children should use it with guidance from a parent or guardian and avoid sharing personal information in support requests. Guardians can contact us with privacy questions. If the release build changes how data is handled, we will update this policy and its effective date.
