Privacy, in plain words.

How Finwords handles learning records, camera images and optional iCloud sync.

Effective date · 17 October 2026

This policy describes Finwords 1.0 for iPhone. We will update it if the app changes how data is handled.

1. Who provides Finwords

Finwords is provided by Codesigner Tech Co., Ltd. and developed by Saber Zou (邹颂兵). For privacy questions or requests about support correspondence, contact saberzou@gmail.com. This policy covers the Finwords iPhone app and these Finwords web pages.

2. Learning records and settings

The app saves your vocabulary, definitions, examples, learning and review state, and associated dates on your device. Your profile includes the names you choose for yourself and your fish, learning level, goals, pace and helper language. Things progress and device preferences are also stored to provide the app’s features. The current build has no Finwords account, developer backend, advertising, tracking, analytics SDK or third-party crash-reporting SDK.

3. Camera images and recognition

Camera access is requested when you use object recognition. Images are processed on your iPhone using Apple frameworks and the bundled TinyCLIP model. The current build does not transmit these images to a developer server or third-party inference service. When you keep a recognised object, the app saves its name, date, optional catalog identifier and cutout image in app storage. Saved images can also sync through iCloud as described below. Camera permission can be changed in iPhone Settings. The camera feature recognises objects, not text.

4. Optional private iCloud sync

When iCloud is available, Finwords uses your private iCloud database to sync words and saved scans, including cutout images. Profile and Things progress use Apple’s iCloud key-value storage. These services depend on your Apple Account, device settings, connectivity and Apple’s service availability. No separate Finwords account is needed, and core learning works locally. Sync can be delayed; keep important information separately rather than relying on sync as a backup. Apple may process data to provide these services.

5. Retention, deletion and export

In Words, open a word card, tap the trash button and confirm to delete that word and its review history. If the word uses a saved scan as its picture, the app also deletes that linked scan record and removes its local picture file and cache. Other separately saved scans remain until removed through Delete all my data. In Me & Fish → Settings, Delete all my data asks for confirmation, then removes words, scans, local photos, profile, Things progress, app settings and pending reminders, and returns to onboarding. Word and scan deletions are sent through CloudKit; profile and Things progress are removed from iCloud key-value storage. Changes on other devices depend on sync and connectivity and may not be immediate. This action does not erase Apple system backups or support emails. There is no complete in-app export feature. Replaying onboarding is not a data reset. Uninstalling alone does not necessarily remove iCloud records or backups. We cannot access your private app records through a developer server. Contact us for help with the available controls.

6. Support email

If you email us, your email address and the content you choose to send are processed by our email service and the developer to respond and resolve the issue. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. Ask us to access, correct or delete personal information in support correspondence; we will consider the request and applicable retention requirements. App records on your device and Apple’s services are separate from these emails.

7. These web pages

These Finwords pages use local assets and system fonts. Their source includes no analytics script, chat widget, tracking cookies or embedded third-party media. The existing website is served through GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. Cloudflare can inject a web-analytics script into the delivered page; these Finwords pages set a Content Security Policy that blocks script execution and outgoing connections, and the injected script was blocked in our publication check. Hosting providers may still process connection information such as your IP address and browser details to serve and secure the website. The main Saber Zou portfolio is a separate page that loads Google Fonts and offers an optional chat service; its network behaviour is not represented by these Finwords pages. Email links open your email application.

8. Children and changes

Finwords has no public profile or communication between users. Children should use it with guidance from a parent or guardian and avoid sharing personal information in support requests. Guardians can contact us with privacy questions. If the release build changes how data is handled, we will update this policy and its effective date.
