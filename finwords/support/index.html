What devices does Finwords support?

Finwords is designed for iPhone running iOS 17 or later. It is coming soon; there is no App Store download link yet.

How does a learning session work?

Your pace sets a daily serving of review words and new words. Catch-up days focus on earlier reviews. You can also ask for more practice. Choice and spelling quizzes help you remember, with spaced review bringing words back over time. Adjust your level and pace in Me & Fish settings.

Does the camera read text?

No. The camera recognises objects and creates cutouts; it is not a text scanner or OCR tool. Recognition can be wrong. Check the suggested name before keeping a thing. You can also look up a word manually.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

There is no Finwords account or sign-in. Core learning and object recognition use bundled data and run on your iPhone. Optional iCloud sync depends on your Apple Account, iCloud settings, service availability and connectivity.

Where are my words and cutouts saved?

Words and cutouts are saved on your device. When iCloud is available, they sync through your private iCloud database; profile and Things progress use iCloud key-value storage. Sync depends on your Apple Account, device settings and connectivity, and can be delayed. Core learning works without an internet connection.

Can I delete or export my data?

To delete a word and its review history, open its card in Words, tap the trash button and confirm. A scan used as that word’s picture is removed too; other separately saved scans remain. To remove all app data, go to Me & Fish → Settings → Delete all my data and confirm. This removes words, scans, local pictures, profile, Things progress, settings and reminders and returns to onboarding. Deletions reach other devices through iCloud when sync is available. Apple system backups and support emails are separate. There is no complete in-app export. Replaying onboarding or uninstalling is not a substitute for deleting synced data.

Why can’t I hear pronunciation?

Check your iPhone volume and available system speech voices. Finwords uses Apple speech synthesis. Tell support which word and language you tried if the problem continues.

Is Finwords free?

The first App Store release is free, with no subscriptions, in-app purchases or advertising.
