A little help for Finwords.

Questions about learning, the camera, or your saved words? Start here, or write to the developer.

What devices does Finwords support?

Finwords is designed for iPhone running iOS 17 or later. It is coming soon; there is no App Store download link yet.

How does a learning session work?

A session can introduce up to five new words, followed by choice and spelling practice. Spaced review revisits words over time. You can adjust your level and pace in Me & Fish settings.

Does the camera read text?

No. The camera recognises objects and creates cutouts; it is not a text scanner or OCR tool. Recognition can be wrong. Check the suggested name before keeping a thing. You can also look up a word manually.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

There is no Finwords account or sign-in. Core learning and object recognition use bundled data and run on your iPhone. Optional iCloud sync depends on your Apple Account, iCloud settings, service availability and connectivity.

Where are my words and cutouts saved?

They are stored on your device. When iCloud is available, the app is configured to sync word records and saved cutouts through your private CloudKit database, and profile and Things progress through iCloud key-value storage. Production and two-device sync are still being verified before release.

Can I delete or export my data?

You can delete an individual word from its word card, after confirmation. The current build has no complete in-app export or Delete All control, and no confirmed saved-cutout deletion control. Removing a word does not delete the separately saved cutout. Uninstalling clears local app storage, but iCloud and device backups may retain copies. Contact support for questions about those copies.

Why can’t I hear pronunciation?

Check your iPhone volume and available system speech voices. Finwords uses Apple speech synthesis. Tell support which word and language you tried if the problem continues.
