A little help for Finwords.

Help with learning, your fish’s diary, saved words, and data carried over from earlier versions.

Email support

Contact Saber Zou at saberzou@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and the steps that led to the problem. Share only what’s needed; avoid sensitive photos or personal information.

Devices and downloads

Finwords supports iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Download it from the App Store; availability may vary by country or region.

Where did Things and Scan go?

In 1.1, Finwords focuses on three tabs: Bowl, Words, and Me & Fish. Things and camera scanning have been removed. Existing words remain, including earlier scan pictures used by those words. Updating does not automatically erase older scan records.

Is the fish’s diary an AI service?

No. It creates short entries on your device from saved learning and review history. It is not a separate online chat or free-form personal journal.

Do I need an account or the internet?

There is no Finwords sign-in. Core learning uses bundled data locally. Optional private iCloud sync needs your Apple Account, appropriate settings and connectivity.

Where is my data / how do I delete it?

See the privacy policy for storage and deletion details. Delete all my data is in Me & Fish → Settings and attempts to clear records and this device’s local data. Successful record deletions can sync, but they do not remotely purge cached pictures, device-only settings or reminders on your other devices. Use the control on each device for local cleanup and check the result. Some saves or file deletions may fail silently; contact support if anything remains. Replaying onboarding and uninstalling are not substitutes for deleting synced data. No complete in-app export is available.

Pricing

See the current App Store listing for pricing and availability.

How does a learning session work?

Your pace sets a daily serving of review words and new words. Catch-up days focus on earlier reviews. You can also ask for more practice. Choice and spelling quizzes help you remember, with spaced review bringing words back over time. Adjust your level and pace in Me & Fish settings.

Why can’t I hear pronunciation?

Check your iPhone volume and available system speech voices. Finwords uses Apple speech synthesis. Tell support which word and language you tried if the problem continues.
