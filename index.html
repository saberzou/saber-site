I love making things. In Beijing I keep building, mostly because it's fun to watch an idea turn into something I can hold.

The apps I enjoyed making most are Movy, BingBang, Sequence, Tackie, Bubbly, Daypond, and Finwords. Walls, drawings, photographs, notes, words, a pond, and a goldfish.

I also like making things for the browser: SaberOS, a desk of wallpapers, and experiments in AI, art, and play. can show you around.
