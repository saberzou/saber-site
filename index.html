Printed for you · Thanksgiving

Thank you.

For wandering in. This room started as a place to keep my work, and somewhere along the way it filled up with toys, a cassette deck and a fish named Archer.

Thank you to everyone who downloaded an app, tried a strange little experiment, wrote to me, or just pressed play on the tape. You keep me making things.

Here’s to more small, odd, joyful things.
