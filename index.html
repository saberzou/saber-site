I'm a designer and builder in Beijing. I make small experiments in AI, art, and play, and seven calm apps for iPhone. No ads. Nothing about you leaves the phone.

Movy for walls, BingBang for a little ease and a few hundred drawings, Sequence for photographs, Tackie for notes, Bubbly for words, Daypond for a quiet pond, and Finwords for a goldfish and a vocabulary.
